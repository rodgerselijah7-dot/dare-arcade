      How to play

      • Tap to jump, hold to go higher, tap again in the air to double jump.
      • Come up short? Runners grab ledges on rooftops and platforms and pull themselves up. Tap while hanging to vault up instantly.
      • Run through crates for an item. Every racer can open every crate once.
      • Special items: Rocket homes in on the runner ahead, Freeze ices everyone behind, Swap trades places with the runner in front, Ghost makes you untouchable for 3s, Magnet pulls in coins.
      • Saws and traps throw ahead with the big button or back with ◀. Bolt zaps everyone, Boost speeds you up, Shield blocks hits.
      • Stages mix loops, bounce walls, tunnels, zip lines, lifts, crumbling bridges, updrafts and laser gates.
      • Six maps: city rooftops plus Frostbite Docks (ice slides, icicles, floes), Magma Works (lava, fireballs, pistons, conveyors) and Jungle Ruins (vine swings, boulders, mushrooms).
      • Grab coins to buy runners, headwear, trails and upgrades. Every runner has a trait and their own entrance at the countdown.
      • ▼ Slide under red beams. In the air it becomes a ground pound that stuns anyone you land near and smashes crates.
      • » Dash bursts forward, once per jump (twice with the jet pack). After a spring or bounce launch it becomes a trick: land clean for a boost, land mid-spin and you stumble.
      • Hit a wall too tall to grab? You wall-slide; tap to wall jump.
      • Dodge: slide, dash or jump just before a rocket, saw or bolt lands to slip past it. Watch for the ! and the arrows at the screen edge.
      • Open a crate while holding an item to level it up (II): twin rockets, triple saws, mirror shields and more. Combos: shield + boost rams people, saw while magnetised homes in, tornado while boosting makes a storm.
      • New items: Grapple, Oil, Tornado, Decoy crates, Time warp and the Crown curse for the leader.
      • Every runner has a ★ special that charges during the race. Tap the ★ button when it glows.
      • Online: quick match, or make a private room and share its 4-letter code. The host picks the track. Join a clan and every race you finish adds to your clan's points.
      • Keyboard: Space jumps, ↓/S slides, D dashes, F special, X or → throws ahead, Z or ← throws back, P pauses.
