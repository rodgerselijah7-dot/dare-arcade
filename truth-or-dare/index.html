How to play

  • Pass the phone. On your turn pick Truth or Dare, or let fate pick.
  • Answer honestly or do the dare, then tap Done it. Truth is +1, dare is +2, and a rare Double Dare is +4.
  • Everyone gets 2 free swaps a game to trade a card for a new one of the same kind.
  • Won't do it? Chicken out for −1.
  • After the last round, the highest score takes the crown. Ties share it.
  • Names on cards are other players. Anything involving someone else only happens if they're up for it.
  • Add your own cards under Your cards. They get shuffled into every deck.

Your cards

Write your own. Use {player} to drop in a random player's name.

Spicy is 18+

Spicy cards are flirty: dates, crushes, kisses on the cheek and slow dances. Everyone playing should be 18 or older, and anything with another player is always their call.
