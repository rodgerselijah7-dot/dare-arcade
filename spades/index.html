How to Play

Teams. You sit across from your partner. Each hand, all 52 cards are dealt, 13 each.

Bidding. Everyone bids how many tricks they expect to win. Your bid plus your partner's is your team's contract. Bid Nil if you think you can take zero tricks.

Blind Nil. If your team is down by 100 or more, you can bid Nil before looking at your cards: +200 if you take no tricks, −200 if you take any.

Play. Follow the suit that was led if you can. If you can't, play anything. A spade trumps every other suit. Highest spade wins, otherwise the highest card of the suit that was led.

Breaking spades. You can't lead spades until a spade has been played on another suit, unless you have nothing but spades.

Scoring. Make your contract: 10 × bid, plus 1 point per extra trick (a "bag"). Miss it: lose 10 × bid. Every 10 bags costs 100 points. Nil: +100 made, −100 busted.

Winning. First team to the target wins. Fall to −200 and you lose.

Controls. Tap a card to lift it, tap again to play. Dimmed cards can't be played right now.
