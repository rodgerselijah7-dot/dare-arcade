HOW TO PLAY
  • Drop five-block shapes and fill whole rows to clear them.
  • Tap the board to rotate · swipe to move · swipe down to drop.
  • Hold a piece once per turn to save it for later.
  • Chain clears for combo multipliers. Quads pay big.
  • Every 5 levels a new zone adds a twist: rising rows, rubble, fog, hidden next.
