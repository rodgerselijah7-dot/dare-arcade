Play full screen

Save the DARE Arcade to your Home Screen. It opens like an app, with no browser bars in the way.

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button in the toolbar. If you don't see the toolbar, tap the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen .
  4. Tap Add in the top-right corner.
  5. Open DARE Arcade from your Home Screen to play full screen.

On newer iPhones, Chrome works too: tap the Share button next to the address bar, then Add to Home Screen.

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu button in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (on some phones it says Install app).
  4. Tap Install or Add to confirm.
  5. Open DARE Arcade from your Home Screen to play full screen.

Samsung Internet: tap the menu button at the bottom, then Add page to, then Home screen.
