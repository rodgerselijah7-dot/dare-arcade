— HOW TO PLAY —
◈
MOVE & FIRE
Drag the joystick to move your vessel.
Hold FIRE to shoot continuously.
Destroy all enemies to advance.
⚡
SPECIAL ABILITY
Tap SP to unleash your ship's power.
Uses 30 energy. Energy refills as you kill.
Each ship has a unique special.
◉
UPGRADES
After most waves, choose an upgrade.
Build your loadout wisely — upgrades
stack and persist through the run.
✦
COSMIC ENCOUNTERS
Every 3rd wave triggers an Encounter —
a mysterious narrative event with choices.
Your decisions reshape the run.
⚠
BOSS WAVES
Every 5th wave is a Boss fight.
Bosses have multiple phases — fire patterns
intensify as their HP drops. Stay mobile.
