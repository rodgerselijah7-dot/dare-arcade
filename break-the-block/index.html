Eighty levels across eight Fort Worth zones. Keep the ball alive, clear every brick, and spend your crowns on upgrades.

By Dontae Amare

Levels

Clear a level to open the next one. A red dot means you've beaten it.

Upgrades

You have 0 crowns

Earn crowns by clearing levels. Gold bricks and lives left over pay extra.

How to play
Drag anywhere to slide the paddle. Your finger doesn't need to be on it. On a laptop, use the mouse or the arrow keys.
Tap (or press space) to launch the ball.

Bricks

Darker bricks take two or three hits.
Steel can't be broken. Play around it.
Red-dot bricks explode and take their neighbors with them.
Gold is worth 500 points and 3 crowns. Bricks marked ? always drop a power-up.
Ghost bricks are nearly invisible until you hit one. Trinity River and beyond.
Regrow bricks heal if you don't finish them within five seconds. Cultural District and beyond.
From Sundance Square on, some rows slide side to side.
