How to Play

Aim: touch or drag on the table to point the cue. Slide the Fine wheel for tiny adjustments.

Shoot: drag the gold knob left to pull back the cue, then let go. Further = harder.

Spin: tap the cue ball button. High = follow, low = draw, left or right = english, which changes how the cue ball comes off the rails.

8-Ball: your first ball sunk after the break makes you Solids (1–7) or Stripes (9–15). Clear your group, then sink the 8.

Tournament: 8-Ball, plus after contact a ball has to sink or hit a rail, and you call the 8: tap the pocket before you shoot it.

9-Ball: always hit the lowest ball first. Sink the 9 on any legal shot, even a combo, to win.

Fouls: scratching, missing everything, or hitting the wrong ball first. Your opponent gets ball in hand and can drag the cue ball anywhere.

Lose instantly in 8-Ball if the 8 drops early, in the wrong pocket, or with a foul.
