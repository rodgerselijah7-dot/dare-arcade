Field guide

Before you shoot

Shot placement

Aim one-third up from the bottom of the chest, right behind the front leg. Lungs run 50 to 100 yards and leave pink, bubbly blood. Liver hits go farther. A paunch hit means waiting, then a long track.

Woodsmanship

  • Wind carries your scent downwind. Watch the flag on screen and keep game upwind.
  • On foot, crouch or crawl as you get close. Moving fast is what gets you seen and heard.
  • Grunt or rattle for bucks during the rut. A predator call brings coyotes fast. Don't overcall.
  • After the shot, an orange flag marks where the animal stood. Follow the blood. Light helps after dark.
  • The feeder goes off before the evening's best light. Watch it.
