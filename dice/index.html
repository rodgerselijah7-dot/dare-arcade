Bets and payouts

The first roll of a round is the come-out. A 4, 5, 6, 8, 9 or 10 on the come-out becomes the point and the puck turns ON. The round ends when the point is rolled again or a 7 shows.

Pass LineCome-out: 7 or 11 wins 1 to 1, 2, 3 or 12 loses. With a point: the point wins 1 to 1, a 7 loses. Place it before the come-out.
Don’t PassThe opposite. Come-out: 2 or 3 wins, 12 pushes, 7 or 11 loses. With a point: a 7 wins 1 to 1, the point loses.
OddsGoes behind your Pass Line once there’s a point, up to 3× your line bet. Pays true odds: 4 or 10 pays 2 to 1, 5 or 9 pays 3 to 2, 6 or 8 pays 6 to 5. The house has no edge on it.
FieldOne roll. 3, 4, 9, 10 or 11 wins 1 to 1, 2 pays 2 to 1, 12 pays 3 to 1. 5, 6, 7 or 8 loses.

On the table

Winning Pass, Don’t Pass and Field bets stay up and you collect the winnings. Odds come back to you with their winnings. Tap a bet to add the chip you picked. Clear takes back every bet you’re allowed to take down.

Online

The shooter rolls for the whole table and keeps the dice until they seven out. Both players bet every roll. Most chips after 30 rolls wins.
