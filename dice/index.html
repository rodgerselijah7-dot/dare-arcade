Scoring

  • Every roll, keep at least one scoring die, then roll the rest or bank your turn total.
  • Kept dice only count when they all score together.
  • Roll with nothing that scores and you bust: the turn total is lost.
  • Keep all six dice and you get hot dice: roll all six again.
  • First to 4,000 wins. If the first player gets there, the other player gets one last turn to beat them.

Leave the match?

The game is still going. Leaving now counts as a loss.
